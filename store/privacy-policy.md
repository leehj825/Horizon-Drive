# Privacy Policy — Horizon Drive

_Last updated: 2026-11-23_

Horizon Drive ("the game") is an offline racing game.

**Data we collect: none.** The game does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The Android app requests **no permissions** and does not use the internet.

**What is stored on your device.** Your progress (credits, upgrades, unlocked content, best times, ghost laps, achievements and settings) is saved locally on your device (browser/WebView local storage). It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this data. The in-game "Reset save" button erases it as well.

**Children.** The game contains no ads, no purchases and no online features, and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this ever changes (for example if online features are added) this policy will be updated before the change ships.

**Contact.** Questions can be sent via the project's GitHub repository issues page: https://github.com/leehj825/Horizon-Drive/issues
